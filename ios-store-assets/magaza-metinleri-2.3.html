What's New — App Store, 4000 characters max (874)

The red gate
Some levels have a second gate beside the path, and it's red. Touch it and the level starts over, along with any stars you'd picked up in it. It used to be white and nobody worked out what it was for.

Slow time
In 37 levels, hold your finger down and time drags. Lift it and the orb launches. The arc around the orb shows how much you have left. These levels are hard.

The gate isn't free any more
It stays shut until you collect at least one star, and it looks shut.

Upside-down levels start at 25, trap gates at 35, inverted colours at 45. All three used to begin after level 150, so most people never saw them.

The tutorial is longer. Rules that were never written down anywhere are explained once now.

Notifications are asked for on first launch. It says what we send, and you can turn it off whenever.

Your stars and finished levels are untouched.
